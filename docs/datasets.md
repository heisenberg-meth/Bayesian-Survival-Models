# Benchmark Datasets

## 1. GBSG2 (German Breast Cancer Study Group)
- **Observations**: 686 patients
- **Event**: Recurrence-free survival time
- **Censoring Rate**: ~56%
- **Key Features**: Age, menopausal status, tumor size, grade, progesterone/estrogen receptors.

## 2. WHAS500 (Worcester Heart Attack Study)
- **Observations**: 500 patients
- **Event**: Mortality following acute myocardial infarction
- **Censoring Rate**: ~57%
- **Key Features**: Age, gender, heart rate, systolic/diastolic blood pressure, BMI.

## 3. METABRIC (Molecular Taxonomy of Breast Cancer International Consortium)
- **Observations**: 1,904 patients
- **Event**: Overall survival duration
- **Censoring Rate**: ~42%
- **Key Features**: Clinical covariates and high-dimensional genomic features.
