# Methodology

## 1. Cox Proportional Hazards Model

The hazard function for subject $i$ at time $t$ is modeled as:

$$
\lambda(t | X_i) = \lambda_0(t) \exp(\beta^T X_i)
$$

where $\lambda_0(t)$ is the baseline hazard and $\beta$ represents log hazard ratios.

## 2. Bayesian Cox Formulation

In the Bayesian framework:

- **Priors**:

  $$
  \beta_k \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_0, \sigma_0^2)
  $$

  $$
  \lambda_0(t) \sim \text{Gamma}(\alpha, \beta) \quad \text{or piecewise constant hazard priors}
  $$
- **Likelihood**:
  Calculated using the partial likelihood formulation or piecewise exponential formulation for right-censored data:

  $$
  L(\beta) = \prod_{i: \delta_i=1} \frac{\exp(\beta^T X_i)}{\sum_{j \in R(t_i)} \exp(\beta^T X_j)}
  $$
- **MCMC Sampling**: Hamiltonian Monte Carlo (HMC) / No-U-Turn Sampler (NUTS) with Gelman-Rubin ($\hat{R}$) convergence checks.

## 3. Spline Baseline Hazard Specification

To overcome the calibration gap of piecewise constant hazards, we specify a continuous baseline hazard using splines.

- **Spline Basis**: We utilize cubic B-splines ($B(t)$) of degree 3 to model the log baseline hazard, ensuring a smooth and continuous hazard function over time.
- **Knot Strategy**: Interior knots are placed at equally spaced quantiles of the observed event times to ensure sufficient data support across all intervals. Boundary knots are placed at $t=0$ and the maximum observed follow-up time.
- **Number of Basis Functions**: We default to a basis dimension of $df = 8$ (which corresponds to 5 interior knots for cubic splines), striking a balance between flexibility and computational stability.
- **Coefficient Prior**: The spline coefficients $\gamma$ follow an independent Normal prior $\gamma_k \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_\gamma, \sigma_\gamma^2)$, where $\mu_\gamma$ is centered on the log of the empirical dataset-wide event rate.
- **Hazard Positivity**: Absolute positivity of the hazard is guaranteed by parameterizing the baseline hazard on the log scale and applying the exponential function:
  $$
  \lambda_0(t) = \exp(B(t)^\top \gamma)
  $$
- **Cumulative Hazard Calculation**: Because the integral of an exponentiated B-spline has no closed-form analytical solution, the cumulative baseline hazard $H_0(t)$ is computed using high-resolution numerical integration (e.g., the trapezoidal rule or Gauss-Legendre quadrature) over a fine grid of time points:
  $$
  H_0(t) = \int_0^t \exp(B(u)^\top \gamma) du
  $$
- **Prediction Mechanism**: For a given patient with covariates $X_i$, the survival probability at time $t$ is generated by numerically evaluating $H_0(t)$ and applying the proportional hazards scaling:
  $$
  S(t \mid X_i) = \exp \left[ -H_0(t) \exp(X_i^\top \beta) \right]
  $$

## 3. Evaluation Metrics

- **Harrell's Concordance Index (C-Index)**: Measures discriminatory power.
- **Brier Score & Integrated Brier Score (IBS)**: Evaluates calibration and prediction accuracy across time.
- **Uncertainty Bounds**: 95% Highest Posterior Density (HPD) intervals for survival probabilities and hazard ratios.
